## Brand & Style

### Narrative & Brand Personality
The design system establishes a warm, grounded, and narrative-first mobile experience centered around the Madeira Petrel (*Freira-da-Madeira*), an endangered seabird nesting in the island's high volcanic peaks. The brand is positioned at the intersection of environmental conservation, sustainable outdoor exploration, and gentle behavioral nudging. Rather than using aggressive gamification or sterile navigation paradigms, the interface feels like an organic field journal: warm, welcoming, patient, and deeply attuned to the natural rhythms of Madeira Island.

### Target Audience & Emotional Arc
Designed for mindful travelers, nature lovers, hikers, and eco-conscious residents who wish to escape overtourism hotspots (such as overcrowded sunrise peaks at Pico do Arieiro) and discover serene, off-the-beaten-path alternatives (such as Ponta do Pargo, Fajã dos Padres, or tucked-away Levadas). The emotional response is intentional:
- **Calm Discovery:** Freedom from crowd anxiety and overstimulation.
- **Empathetic Purpose:** Direct contribution to bird conservation and local micro-economies.
- **Rewarding Accomplishment:** Quiet satisfaction as collected petrel feathers (*penas*) unlock local bakery, café, and trail badges.

### Visual Style
A hybrid of **Warm Tactile Minimalism** and **Storybook Vector Illustration**. The aesthetic relies on earthy island mineral tones, organic rounded card geometries, generous touch targets, subtle tonal layering rather than heavy synthetic shadows, and hand-drawn line accents that celebrate Madeiran topography, coastal cliffs, and subtropical laurel forests.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The palette takes direct provenance from Madeira’s endemic ecosystem: the Atlantic Ocean swell, the mist-draped volcanic peaks, the deep UNESCO Laurisilva canopy, and the sun-baked terracotta clay of coastal villages.

### Primary Roles & Functional Mapping
- **Primary (`#1E5F8B` - Atlantic Deep):** Primary interaction surfaces, key CTAs, navigational wayfinding accents, active mission states, and major brand iconography. Communicates oceanic calm, navigational reliability, and depth.
- **Secondary (`#6DA65D` - Laurel Sage):** Eco-achievements, crowd-free status indicators ("Calm / Tranquilo"), route completion confirmations, and positive reinforcement markers. Complemented by its darker companion (`#1F4A2C` - Laurisilva Pine) for high-contrast environmental badges and dense data headers.
- **Tertiary (`#BF5841` - Terracotta Clay):** Highlighting dynamic incentives (such as "x2 Points" multipliers), crowd warning banners, milestone feather counters, and local partner merchant incentives (village bakeries, artisanal coffee stops).
- **Neutral Core (`#3B4E59` - Slate Basalt):** Used for typography, crisp iconography outlines, and grounded borders. It avoids harsh pitch black (`#000000`), preserving the soft, organic atmosphere.
- **Background & Containers:** 
  - Primary canvas: `#F9F7F2` (Warm Parchment)
  - Secondary card surface / sunken container: `#F2EFE9` (Sand Dune)
  - Elevated floating surfaces: `#FFFFFF` (Cloud White)

## Typography

### Typographic Hierarchy
**Plus Jakarta Sans** is selected across all roles to ensure geometric balance, open apertures, and warm, rounded terminals that mirror the petrel bird illustration style. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Used for mission invitations ("Arieiro is crowded! Help Freira?"), stage celebrations, and route milestones. Heavy weights (700 and 800) maintain visual grounding against illustrated landscape backdrops.
- **Body:** Open counters ensure effortless readability in direct sunlight along outdoor mountain trails.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High-contrast, semi-bold weights for rapid glanceability on maps, GPS check-in prompts, status tags, and QR scanning instructions.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy
A mobile-first touch architecture with adaptive single-column flow. Because the app is predominantly operated with one hand while on trail walks or navigating transport hubs, all interactive cards, bottom sheets, and action triggers are positioned within the lower two-thirds of the viewport ("thumb zone").

### Spacing Rhythm
- Built on a strict 4px/8px incremental base unit.
- **Outer Canvas Margins:** 16px on compact mobile viewports, expanding to 20px on standard phablets.
- **Component Padding:** Standard cards utilize `1rem` (16px) internal padding, with compact chips using `0.5rem` (8px) horizontal padding.
- **Bottom Navigation Dock:** Fixed 80px elevated pill or floating sheet with safe-area padding for system bars.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Tonal Depth
Visual hierarchy avoids harsh drop shadows, opting for natural light diffusion reflecting Madeira's marine cloud canopy (*mar de nuvens*).

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F9F7F2` parchment fill, flat.
- **Level 1 (Surface Containers & Mission Cards):** `#FFFFFF` fill with a subtle 1px border (`#E4DFD5`) and an ambient, warm tinted shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(59, 78, 89, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Map Drawers & Modals):** `#FFFFFF` fill with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 95, 139, 0.12)`, grounding user attention on critical decisions (e.g., "Accept Alternate Mission").
- **Level 3 (Floating System Elements):** Bottom sticky CTA bars and floating map centerpins feature crisp `#3B4E59` micro-borders (0.5px at 15% opacity) to guarantee edge definition over satellite and topographic terrain layers.

## Shapes

### Shape Architecture
A level `2` rounded geometry is implemented throughout the design system. The softened 16px to 24px (`rounded-lg` and `rounded-xl`) outer corners evoke natural pebbles, coastal stones, and the round silhouette of the Freira petrel mascot.

- **Primary Interactive Cards:** `rounded-lg` (16px) creates a friendly, tactile boundary.
- **Action Buttons & Badges:** `rounded-xl` (24px) up to full pill form (`9999px`) for touch targets, ensuring comfortable thumb contact and visual friendliness.
- **Embedded Story Illustrations & Viewport Windows:** `rounded-lg` (16px) bounding frames with delicate soft-colored inner matting.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Accept Mission", "Check In with GPS"):** Solid `#1E5F8B` background, `#FFFFFF` text, 48px minimum height, pill-shaped radius (`9999px`), active press state scaling down to `0.98` with darkened background `#174A6D`.
- **Secondary Action ("Alternative Routes"):** Outlined `#3B4E59` (1.5px border), `#F9F7F2` background, slate text.
- **Incentive Button ("x2 Points / Calm Route"):** `#BF5841` background with white text or tinted sage `#6DA65D` with dark green text for ecological milestones.

### Cards
- **Mission Card:** Clean container on `#FFFFFF`, framed with a 1px border of `#E5E0D8`. Top section hosts the location illustration (e.g., Ponta do Pargo Lighthouse, Fajã dos Padres cable house), followed by title, crowd status tag, feather progress indicator (`3 / 10 Feathers`), and direct inline action.
- **Crowd Alert Banner:** Tinted `#BF5841` surface at 10% opacity, featuring slate navy text and the Freira mascot speaking in conversational tone ("Arieiro is crowded right now! Help Freira find peace?").

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chip ("Calm"):** Background `#E8F2E4`, text `#1F4A2C`, pill-shaped, prepended with a soft leaf or feather vector icon.
- **Status Chip ("Crowded"):** Background `#FDEEEB`, text `#BF5841`, advising redirection.
- **Multiplier Pill ("x2 Points"):** Pill chip with `#EBF4F9` background, `#1E5F8B` typography, bold label styling.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Search & Filter Bars:** Pill-shaped text fields with `#F2EFE9` backgrounds, `#3B4E59` placeholder text, and leading search icons.
- **Progress Trackers (Feather Collector Bar):** Track bar filled with `#E5E0D8`, progress filled with animated `#BF5841` (clay orange) capped with rounded terminals, representing collected petrel feathers.

### Map & Check-In Modules
- **Location Check-In Card:** Bottom-docked sheet containing validation mechanism (GPS match checkmark or QR Code scan button) paired with immediate celebratory reward feedback ("+1 Feather & +40 Points earned!").
- **Reward QR Voucher Card:** High-contrast coupon with dashed border divider, presenting local café redeemables (e.g., "15% off artisanal coffee & bolo do caco").
